Privacy Policy

Zintara · Last updated: 27 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to all mobile games published by Zintara on Google Play (package names beginning with com.zintara), including Jar of Goofs, Stack-a-Freak, Moth to the Lamp, Snack Slasher, Gobble Gus, Sort the Horde, Moon Chaos, Steal a Jugaad, 67 Reflex, Merge Mohalla, Escape the Monsoon, Aura Farming Idle, Rank the Rot, Brainrot Fusion Lab, Guess the Sound or Get Yeeted, Bazaar Tower Defense, and Meme Reset 2014. Our games are designed for everyone, including children, and we take children's privacy seriously.

What we collect — in short

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any account.
  • Game progress (scores, unlocks, settings) is stored only on your device and never sent to us.
  • The games show ads through Google AdMob, which processes limited device data to serve ads (see below).
  • We, Zintara, run no servers and receive no personal data from you.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our games are free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Because our games may appeal to children, every ad request is tagged as child-directed under Google's policies: ads are non-personalized, ad content is limited to a G (general audiences) rating, and personalized-advertising identifiers are not used.

To serve and measure ads, Google may process limited device information (such as IP address and non-persistent device identifiers) for purposes permitted for child-directed services, like frequency capping, fraud prevention, and aggregate reporting. You can read how Google uses this data here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & data protection
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services

Children's privacy

Our games do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There are no accounts, no chat, no social features, and no free-text input. Ads are configured for child-directed treatment as described above, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR, and the Google Play Families policy. If you believe a child has somehow provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Purchases and rewarded ads

Our games currently contain no in-app purchases. Some games offer optional rewarded ads (for example, watching a video to retry a round). These are always opt-in and clearly labelled.

Data deletion

All game data lives on your device. Uninstalling a game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes it. We hold no copy.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change what data is processed, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above before releasing those features.
